I am a CSC-funded postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Manchester, working with Lasse Rempe. My research is in complex analysis, complex differential equations and transcendental dynamics.

I obtained my Ph.D. in pure mathematics from Shantou University in 2025, advised by Hasi Wulan and Zhi-Tao Wen. I was an academic visitor at Kiel University during 2023–2024, hosted by Walter Bergweiler.

I am also exploring AI-assisted formalization in Lean of mathematics related to complex analysis.

AI-assisted mathematical formalization

I use AI-assisted formalization in Lean for mathematics related to complex analysis. Each project records the precise statements, source versions and verification results.

  • Li–Wang–Wen: representation of finite-order solutions

    Verified Theorems 3.1, 3.2 and 3.5, Corollaries 3.3 and 3.4, Lemmas 5.1 and 5.2, supplementary Lemma 5.2′, and the complete conclusions of Examples 3.8 and 3.9. 101 modules and 729 audited declarations.

    Citation: X.-Y. Li, Lean formalization of the Li–Wang–Wen representation theorems, v0.1.1 (2026).

    Fixed source · v0.1.1Verification reportCitation

  • CRG: growth and zero distribution

    Verified all seven numbered theorem, proposition and corollary statements of the preprint. Version 1.0.2 also independently derives exact order one, finite positive type and completely regular growth from the actual equation with constant-multiplier exponential-sum coefficients. 327 modules and 1,752 audited declarations.

    Citation: X.-Y. Li, Lean formalization of completely regular growth for differential equations, v1.0.2 (2026).

    Fixed source · v1.0.2Verification reportCitationEarlier v1.0.1 · Zenodo DOI
