A figure leaves the paper

The convex hull on this page comes from a figure in my work on complex differential equations. There, it helps describe which exponential term dominates in a given direction.

Here, the same geometry becomes a way to explore my work. A vertex leads to a section; the spaces between sections leave room for these smaller notes. One figure, with another life outside the paper.

A page from Liverpool

At the Holomorphic Dynamics Scheme 3 meeting in Liverpool, I gave a talk on finite-order solutions of complex linear differential equations with exponential-polynomial coefficients.

The title is long, but the starting point fits in a question: how much can the coefficients tell us about the growth of a solution? The talk moved from that question to exponential sums, sectorial asymptotics and completely regular growth.
