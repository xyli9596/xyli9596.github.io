About me

I am a CSC-funded postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Manchester, working with Lasse Rempe. My research is in complex analysis, complex differential equations and transcendental dynamics.

I obtained my Ph.D. in pure mathematics from Shantou University in 2025, advised by Hasi Wulan and Zhi-Tao Wen. I was an academic visitor at Kiel University during 2023–2024, hosted by Walter Bergweiler.

I am also exploring AI-assisted formalization in Lean of mathematics related to complex analysis.
