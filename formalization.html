05 / Experiments with Lean

Formalization

I am exploring AI-assisted formalization in Lean of mathematics related to complex analysis. These are experimental projects; correspondence between the formal statements and the source mathematics remains to be systematically reviewed.

Li–Wang–Wen: representation of finite-order solutions

Verified Theorems 3.1, 3.2 and 3.5, Corollaries 3.3 and 3.4, Lemmas 5.1 and 5.2, supplementary Lemma 5.2′, and the complete conclusions of Examples 3.8 and 3.9. 101 modules and 729 audited declarations.

Citation: X.-Y. Li, Lean formalization of the Li–Wang–Wen representation theorems, v0.1.1 (2026).

Fixed source · v0.1.1 ↗Verification report ↗Citation ↗

CRG: growth and zero distribution

Verified all seven numbered theorem, proposition and corollary statements of the preprint. Version 1.0.2 also independently derives exact order one, finite positive type and completely regular growth from the actual equation with constant-multiplier exponential-sum coefficients. 327 modules and 1,752 audited declarations.

Citation: X.-Y. Li, Lean formalization of completely regular growth for differential equations, v1.0.2 (2026).
